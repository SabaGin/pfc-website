Meet Priscilla

Ciao, I am Priscilla — an Italian mum living in Thailand for 9 years.

I started PFC because I know how overwhelming it can be to plan the perfect family holiday — endless tabs, forum threads, and reviews that never quite tell you what you need to know.

With four kids of my own, aged 2 to 13, I've tested more resorts, beaches, and "kid-friendly" claims than I can count — and I know just as well what makes a trip work for couples looking for quiet, romantic time away. Every PFC itinerary comes from what I'd genuinely choose for my own family, or for a night out with my husband.
